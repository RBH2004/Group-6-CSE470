# BrandTruth demo walkthrough

## C1 – Unified Product Search & Price Comparison
1. Open the home page.
2. Use the search bar to look for "Samsung" or "rice".
3. Open `/search` and test the category and channel filters.
4. Click a product card to view the product detail page and compare prices by channel.

## C2 – Price History & Buy Timing Indicator
1. Open a product detail page.
2. Review the verified price history and the buy-timing pill.
3. Confirm the current price is flagged as Good, Fair, or High based on the recent bound.

## C3 – Fake Discount Checker
1. Open `/discount-checker`.
2. Enter a claimed original price and a current price.
3. Review the verdict: Genuine, Inflated, or Insufficient data.
4. Check the evidence block for the highest verified price, channel, and date.

## C4 – Price Submission with Evidence
1. Go to `/submit-price`.
2. Fill in a product, store, date, and price.
3. Try a duplicate submission to confirm the 24-hour duplicate guard blocks it.
4. Review the entry in `/my-submissions`.

## C5 – Community Verification
1. Open `/verification-feed`.
2. Confirm or dispute a pending submission.
3. Watch the progress bar move toward the 3.0 threshold.
4. Observe the vote summary change state to Verified or Disputed.

## C6 – Watchlist & Price-Drop Alerts
1. Open `/watchlist`.
2. Review the active alert target and status.
3. Use the search flow to create a new target-price alert.
4. Confirm the alert becomes triggered when the price falls below the target and can re-arm later.

## C7 – Concern Resolution
1. Open `/concerns`.
2. Open a concern thread.
3. Review the message bubbles and status labels.
4. Confirm the thread is demo-ready for the brand response flow.

## C8 – Reviews & Ratings
1. Visit `/reviews`.
2. Review the rating distribution and value-for-money comments.
3. Add a new review and observe the updated product feedback summary.

## C9 – Smart Basket Optimizer
1. Open `/basket`.
2. Review the best single-store plan and split-across-stores plan.
3. Confirm that the savings amount is highlighted and the total matches the hand calculation for the demo products.
